# Prompts das artes originais

Ferramenta: geração de imagens integrada, sem chave de API no projeto. Os arquivos finais estão em `apps/web/public/assets`. Não é necessário gerar imagens novamente para executar o jogo.

## Cenário (`art/classroom.png`)

Create one 16:9 landscape pixel art background illustration for a classroom web game called Impostor TI. No UI, no words, no text or letters, no logos, no characters. Cozy school computer science lab in a high quality detailed 16-bit videogame style, clearly pixelated edges, warm sunset light through large windows at the left, wooden desks, a few desktop computers along the back wall, bookshelves and books at the right, potted plants, small globe at right, chalkboard in the back, science classroom details. Warm orange edges and deep navy blue / muted purple center. The middle 65 percent must be very dark calm and uncluttered, suitable behind large readable game panels. Match a charming polished pixel art classroom social deduction party game. Full bleed background, straight on view, no blank outer border.

## Personagens (`art/avatars.png`)

One sprite sheet atlas for an original classroom pixel-art party game. EXACT GRID OF 4 COLUMNS AND 6 ROWS, 24 different young adult cute school gamer characters. Each cell is exactly the same size, fully transparent background, no checkerboard no text no labels no numbers no border. Each character centered in its cell at the same position, full body, enough empty padding around every cell. Tiny charming 16-bit pixel art sprites with crisp square pixels, facing front, large heads small bodies, black pixel outlines, neutral happy friendly expressions, caps, t-shirts, trousers and sneakers. Characters in same base pose but diverse skin tones and hair, and 24 distinct combinations of red, blue, green, yellow, orange, purple, cyan, pink and white cap and shirt colors. Consistent style and scale. Entire sheet is square 1024 by 1024, evenly divided into exactly 4 columns and 6 rows. No shadows outside character, no props, no background. This is a single game sprite atlas asset.

O resultado efetivo tem 1254×1254 pixels e margens entre as colunas; os enquadramentos CSS foram ajustados ao arquivo recebido, preservando o original transparente.
